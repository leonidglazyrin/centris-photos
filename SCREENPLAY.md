# THE LAST LANTERN

*A love story in blocks — running time 5:00*

Written by Claude

---

## CAST

**JUNO**: A lantern maker in the lakeside village of Willow Lake. Copper hair, a flower behind her ear and a sage-green apron. Practical and warm, with a lot left unsaid.

**ROWAN**: A wandering cartographer with a navy coat, a red scarf and a satchel full of unfinished maps. Has never stayed anywhere long enough to call it home.

---

## ACT ONE: THE VILLAGE THAT WASN'T ON THE MAP

**FADE IN:**

**1. EXT. WILLOW LAKE VALLEY — DAWN** *(0:00)*

Mist lies on a glassy lake. A square sun climbs over blocky mountains. The camera cranes slowly down from the clouds toward a small village of timber cottages and a long wooden pier lined with lantern posts.

> TITLE: **THE LAST LANTERN**

**2. EXT. THE PIER — EARLY MORNING** *(0:14)*

A wide shot. JUNO walks the length of the pier carrying a fresh lantern and checks each post as she passes. Every post has a lantern except the last one.

> SUPER: *Willow Lake. Spring.*

**3. EXT. END OF THE PIER — CONTINUOUS** *(0:24)*

A close shot. Juno stops at the empty post at the very end and touches the bare hook.

> **JUNO** *(to herself)*
> Grandma always said the last post waits...
> for someone worth lighting it for.

**4. EXT. THE LAKE — MORNING** *(0:36)*

Out in the mist a small wooden boat takes shape. ROWAN is rowing, badly, and squints at a map.

**5. EXT. THE PIER — MOMENTS LATER** *(0:46)*

The boat bumps the pier. Rowan climbs out and nearly falls in.

> **ROWAN**
> Hi! Is this... Willow Lake? It isn't on any map.

> **JUNO**
> Then your map is wrong.

**6. EXT. THE PIER — CONTINUOUS** *(0:58)*

A two-shot. Rowan unrolls a blank-edged map.

> **ROWAN**
> I'm Rowan. I draw maps.

> **JUNO**
> Juno. I make lanterns.

> **ROWAN**
> Then maybe you can help me find my way.

---

## ACT TWO: A SEASON OF SMALL THINGS

*The music swells into a montage.*

**7. EXT. CHERRY GROVE — MIDDAY** *(1:10)*

The camera orbits slowly as pink petals drift down. Juno leads Rowan between the cherry trees.

> **JUNO**
> This is the grove. Every tree here has a name.

**8. EXT. THE HILL — AFTERNOON** *(1:24)*

Rowan sketches on the hilltop while Juno points out the valley. The camera cranes up to show the lake below.

> **ROWAN**
> Rivers, ridges, roads... I can map all of it.

> **JUNO**
> Not all of it.

**9. INT./EXT. JUNO'S WORKSHOP — LATE AFTERNOON** *(1:36)*

At the workbench Juno shows Rowan how to build a lantern. Rowan holds one up, a lopsided little thing, glowing.

> **ROWAN**
> Mine looks like a potato.

> **JUNO** *(laughing)*
> A glowing potato. It's perfect.

**10. EXT. THE HILL — GOLDEN HOUR** *(1:48)*

Together they plant a single cherry sapling on the crest of the hill. The camera dollies slowly past them.

**11. EXT. THE HILL — SUNSET** *(2:00)*

Two silhouettes sit side by side as the sun sinks into the lake.

> **ROWAN**
> The map's almost finished.

> **JUNO**
> ...And then you leave.

> **ROWAN**
> Cartographers always leave. It's the job.

*A long silence. The sun is gone.*

**12. EXT. THE PIER — DUSK** *(2:20)*

The pier lanterns flicker on one by one. Juno stands alone. The last post is still dark.

---

## ACT THREE: THE LIGHT AT THE END OF THE PIER

**13. EXT. THE PIER — BLUE HOUR, DAWN** *(2:36)*

A low angle through the mist. Rowan's boat pulls away.

> **ROWAN**
> I'll come back.

> **JUNO**
> Everyone says that.

The boat dissolves into the fog.

**14. INT. JUNO'S WORKSHOP — NIGHT** *(2:52)*

By candlelight Juno builds a new lantern, the finest she has ever made.

**15. EXT. END OF THE PIER — NIGHT** *(3:04)*

Juno hangs the lantern on the last post and lights it. The warm glow blooms across the water.

> **JUNO**
> So you can find your way back.

**16. EXT. WILLOW LAKE — WINTER** *(3:16)*

> SUPER: *Winter.*

Snow falls on the rooftops, the grove and the hill. Juno trudges down the snowy pier with the lantern still burning.

**17. EXT. END OF THE PIER — WINTER NIGHT** *(3:34)*

Juno sits alone at the end of the pier beneath the last lantern and watches the dark water. Snow settles on her shoulders.

**18. EXT. WILLOW LAKE — SPRING NIGHT** *(3:46)*

> SUPER: *Spring.*

The blossoms are back and fireflies drift over the reeds. Far out on the lake a tiny light appears. Juno rises to her feet.

**19. EXT. THE PIER — CONTINUOUS** *(4:00)*

The boat glides in. It is Rowan, a little weathered, holding a rolled map.

> **ROWAN**
> I finished the map.

*Insert: the map. The whole valley is drawn with care, and at its center, beside a tiny pier, one word is written: HOME.*

> **ROWAN**
> Turns out every road on it leads here.

**20. EXT. END OF THE PIER — CONTINUOUS** *(4:16)*

> **JUNO**
> You followed the lantern.

> **ROWAN**
> I followed you.

They embrace. Fireflies swirl, and from the village sky lanterns begin to rise.

**21. EXT. WILLOW LAKE — NIGHT** *(4:30)*

The camera cranes up from the two figures on the pier, past the rising lanterns and into a sky full of stars. On the hill, the sapling they planted has blossomed.

**FADE OUT.**
